NOVA Sample Employee Handbook (demo document for RAG)

Vacation and time off
Employees accrue 20 paid vacation days per calendar year. Unused days roll
over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next year. Requests longer than one
week require manager approval submitted at least 14 days in advance. Paid
sick leave accrues separately at 10 days per year and does not roll over.
Parental leave is 16 weeks at full pay.

Remote work
Remote work requires a signed equipment agreement before the first workday
from home. Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and the corporate
VPN. The office is closed between Christmas and New Year's Eve.

Security rules
All passwords must be at least 12 characters long. Passwords expire every
90 days and must never be reused. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory
for email, the HR portal, and any production system. Lost devices must be
reported to IT within one hour of discovery.

Travel
Travel reimbursements must be filed within 30 days of returning from a
trip. Economy class flights are standard for hops under six hours.
